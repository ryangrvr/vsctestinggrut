## 5. Per-primitive requirement

Every new primitive must carry: exact mathematical definition; physical interpretation;
information price; law-data vs state-data classification; representation invariance;
composition behavior; empirical bridge; known-theory comparator; failure condition.

## 6. Law versus state (central distinction)

A context/access structure could be: **universal law structure** (same in every world) /
**solution-state structure** (varies between worlds under a smaller universal law) /
**boundary-initial structure** (selects a history) / **representational** (not physical).
A theory is not required to derive all state data — **failure to derive the exact realized
`C` is NOT automatically a failure**. The real burden is identifying a small universal law
that constrains admissible `C` — and that law is F0-B, out of scope. PHYS-02 identifies
what such a future law would have to act ON.
