## 1. Purpose

Determine what a GGC0 generative law would be *obligated* to explain, before
inventing any such law. This charter classifies each surviving supplied input
(designated below as "the Ledger") into one of three bins:

- **LAW-LIKE**
- **SOLUTION-LIKE**
- **MIXED / UNRESOLVED**

The bins are defined in §3. The Ledger is identified in §5. The classification
itself is NOT part of this document.

## 3. The classification criterion (the only operative content of this charter)

**Ratification status (AMENDMENT 01, 2026-10-04).** The owner ruled
AMEND-BEFORE-APPROVAL: this criterion is not ratified at commit `41a1794`.
The amendments below (classification grain = atomic A-1…A-16; Q1 admissible
variation; Q2 theory-specification test; Q3 precedent discipline; §4
demoted from operative rule to reference) implement that ruling. **G1 is NOT
authorized by this document**; classification begins only after the owner
approves the amended criterion.

**Grain of classification.** The canonical units of G1 are the sixteen
atomic supplied inputs **A-1 through A-16** (`GRUT_WORKING_THEORY_SYNTHESIS_01.md`
§1), each classified separately. The nine L1–L9 layers of
`GGC0_RESIDUAL_REGISTER_01.md` are architectural roll-ups, not classification
units. Relationships between items may be recorded, but no two A-n items may
be merged before classification; layer-level summaries may be produced only
after all atomic classifications are frozen, and never overwrite them.

For each item I, ask exactly three questions, in order. The answers,
not intuition, fix the bin.

**Q1 (Admissible-variation test).** Holding every other supplied input fixed
insofar as mathematically consistent, and preserving all earned constraints
and the recorded model class: can I be varied so that the result remains an
admissible instance of the same governing structure?
- Yes, and the variation selects a different admissible state, history,
  boundary condition, preparation, sector or solution without changing the
  governing rule → **SOLUTION-LIKE**.
- Changing I changes the governing rule or admissible structure itself →
  **LAW-LIKE**.
- I cannot be varied independently without leaving the recorded class or
  jointly changing another residual item → **INDETERMINATE**. Non-independence
  may not by itself be used as evidence for LAW-LIKE.

**Q2 (Theory-specification test).** Can one unchanged specification of the
theory admit two physically admissible instances that differ in I, with the
difference supplied only when a particular solution/history is selected?
- Yes → **SOLUTION-LIKE**.
- Specifying I is required to define the equations, admissible state space,
  dynamical map, symmetry/causal structure, measurement/access structure, or
  other rule determining what counts as a permissible solution in the first
  place → **LAW-LIKE**.
- The record cannot decide this without assuming the conclusion →
  **INDETERMINATE**.

**Q3 (Precedent test — advisory, cannot overrule Q1/Q2).** Every precedent
answer must name the comparator theory and explain the matched scope. Where
comparably appropriate accepted theories provide conflicting precedents, Q3
is recorded **AMBIGUOUS**. No favorable comparator may be selected merely
because it supports a desired bin.

**Bin rules.**
- Q1 LAW-LIKE + Q2 LAW-LIKE → **LAW-LIKE** (Q3 conflict noted).
- Q1 SOLUTION-LIKE + Q2 SOLUTION-LIKE → **SOLUTION-LIKE** (Q3 conflict noted).
- Any disagreement or INDETERMINATE answer → **MIXED / UNRESOLVED**.
  No tie-breaking judgment is permitted in execution. MIXED is a real bin
  and may be a final classification.

**Explicit prohibitions during classification.**
- No item may be moved to SOLUTION-LIKE because moving it would help GGC0
  (or to LAW-LIKE because it would help a candidate law). Motive-blindness
  is the point of freezing this criterion.
- No re-grading of the underlying earned results; the Ledger items enter with
  their recorded statuses.
- No merging of items that the Ledger records as distinct, and no splitting
  of items the record treats as one — the unit of classification is the
  record's own unit.
- Every classification must cite the source record for the item's current
  status AND give one adversarial counterargument to the assigned bin. A
  classification without a recorded counterargument is invalid.
