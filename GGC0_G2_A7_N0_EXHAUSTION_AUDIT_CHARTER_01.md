> A deterministic F may itself select or uniquely privilege a physical,
> invariant, SRB-like, equivariant, ergodic or otherwise canonical measure
> through its dynamics/geometry, rather than requiring an independently
> supplied initial-condition measure.

1. an externally inserted measure;
2. an invariant measure merely compatible with F;

   basin of initial states;
5. an equivariant measure determined uniquely within a declared
   functional/locality class.
