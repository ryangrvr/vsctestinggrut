## 1. Error acknowledgment (recorded at the owner's direction)

The owner's earlier Frontier A input listed "two parties with three or more settings"
as a route to quantum strong contextuality, sourced from a review article's sentence
that put the resource in the wrong place: **settings are not the resource**.
Brassard–Méthot–Tapp show that with a qubit on either side, no number of measurements
produces strong nonlocality; what matters is **local dimension, with 3×3 the minimum**.
The owner also quoted a sentence ("provably no bipartite quantum-realisable behaviour
of this kind…") whose scope, as the verification found, is wider than its own
citations support. **Both the correction and the provenance note stand** (cite
Abramsky–Brandenburger Proposition 6.2 and Mansfield's published proof, not the
introduction).

## 3. Translation requirement (confirmed; folded into the charter as CR3)

(C, Γ) contains no Hilbert dimension. Any dimension-like structure a candidate law
uses must be **declared and priced** (R10). Capacity (maximum perfectly distinguishable
local states) is an insufficient stand-in: **a qubit and a boxworld gbit both have
capacity 2, yet one forbids strong contextuality in (2,2,2) and the other produces PR
boxes** (boxworld: Barrett, arXiv:quant-ph/0508211 — "states that can give rise to
arbitrary non-signalling correlations, including … Popescu-Rohrlich boxes", verified
at abstract level; the "gbit"/capacity-2 framing is standard GPT literature,
owner-stated). What separates them is the structure of the local state space
(Janotta–Gogolin–Barrett–Brunner) — which is exactly why the capacity hostile control
(SD-K8) and the local-geometry comparator exist, and why passing A4 + A-BQ requires
the law to produce or respect local-system structure **without** importing it under
§3's prohibitions.

The frozen charter's claim that all SD-K controls are "exact and finite" conflicted
with the universal scopes in SD-K4 (GHZ for all n ≥ 3) and SD-K7 (the qubit-side
theorem, universal over scenarios and measurements). The scientific gates are fine;
the scopes are normalized:

- **CR1 — SD-K4:** the finite pass/fail gate is **three-qubit GHZ** ((3,2,2), total
  dimension 2×2×2 = 8). Larger n is an **extension check**, not part of the finite
  gate.
- **CR2 — SD-K7:** a **finite representative hostile control** (a declared finite
  family of qubit-side bipartite support patterns the law must FORBID) **plus the BMT
  universal theorem-boundary audit** (the law's claimed exclusion is compared against
  the POVM-inclusive BMT theorem scope as an audit obligation, not a finite
  computation).
