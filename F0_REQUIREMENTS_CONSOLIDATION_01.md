## 4. Frontier decision (owner ruling, 2026-10-06)

Exactly two real candidate frontiers:

- **A. SUPPORT DETERMINATION** — physical/task structure → Supp. The qualitative law.
  G0 explicitly did **not** derive it.
- **B. WITHIN-SUPPORT WEIGHT SELECTION** — Supp → Γ. Closer to the Born/statistical
  problem; likely harder.

**Decision: A first, after this consolidation.** Rationale (owner): G0 showed support is
the first level at which qualitative possibility can materially reduce statistical
freedom — some supports force a unique empirical model. If the theory cannot generate
the zero structure at all, there is no reason to expect it to generate precise positive
weights. **No campaign is opened by this artifact**; opening the support-determination
campaign requires its own charter and owner authorization, and remains subject to the
F0-B prohibition and every requirement above.
