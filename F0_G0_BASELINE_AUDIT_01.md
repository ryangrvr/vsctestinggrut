# F0 G0 — BASELINE AUDIT 01
**Status:** campaign artifact. Targeted comparison against the frameworks that already
connect possibility/compatibility structure to probability. NOT FOUND ≠ DOES NOT EXIST.

## 1. Sheaf-theoretic empirical models (Abramsky–Brandenburger, arXiv:1006.0484)

- **What it supplies:** measurement covers, compatible families of distributions
  (empirical models), support, global/hidden-section hierarchy; the admissible-set
  machinery G0 computes is exactly the standard empirical-model polytope (normalization +
  no-disturbance + support zeros) over the cover.
- **Is G0 merely reconstructing the empirical-model polytope?** **Yes, at levels G0-B/G0-C:
  the computation is the standard one.** The G0-specific contributions are (a) the exact
  affine-dimension bookkeeping per input level, and (b) the identifiability *framing* —
  asking how much the static structure determines, as a graded ladder — not new
  mathematics.

## 2. Possibilistic contextuality (support-level obstruction)

- **What it supplies:** "possibility" as support membership; contextuality as absence of a
  global section consistent with support (the possibilistic/local orthogonality style).
  The K2 anti-correlated support finding (unique model, no global section over the triple)
  is exactly a support-level obstruction familiar from this literature.
- **What support determines without weights:** which section combinations are jointly
  compatible; zero patterns; *not* positive weights.

## 3. Exclusivity / consistent exclusivity

- Exclusivity relations among events (distinct outcomes of one context are exclusive;
  exclusivity groups across contexts) generate probability inequalities (e.g. CHSH-type,
  exclusivity bounds). **Do not conflate exclusivity structure with compatibility
  structure** — they are different event-relation layers (G0-D vs the cover structure).
  Audited: exclusivity imposes inequalities once the exclusive structure is *supplied*;
  it does not derive the structure from C.

## 4. Gleason-type results (the strongest warning comparator)

- **Gleason (and its variants)** derive the probability measure from additivity over the
  projective lattice — but **only after supplying the Hilbert-space/projective geometry
  and the additivity assumption**. The probability structure is bought with the entire
  geometric apparatus. For G0's purposes: classified as **G0-E extra structure** — a
  demonstration that rich probability constraints require rich supplied geometry, not a
  derivation from C. (Citation per owner: arXiv:2205.00493 as a modern Gleason-context
  reference.)

## 5. GPT / effect-algebra frameworks

- State/effect cones, effect algebras: probability rules come from the supplied cone/
  algebra geometry. Again G0-E: the structure that constrains statistics must be supplied
  wholesale.

## 6. Constructor theory of probability (Marletto, arXiv:1507.03287 / Proc. R. Soc. A 471
## (superseded id corrected earlier: 20150883), DOI 10.1098/rspa.2015.0883)

- Audited (also in PHYS-02): unpredictability defined via possible/impossible tasks;
  decision-supporting weights derived **only under substantial superinformation-theory
  conditions**. It does **not** derive ordinary context-indexed statistics from a bare
  compatibility hypergraph. **Do not say CT derives ordinary Γ merely because it derives
  decision weights under additional conditions.**

## 7. Possibility vs zero probability (charter §9 — audited per framework)

| framework | impossible ⇒ p=0 | p=0 ⇒ impossible? |
|---|---|---|
| empirical models (support machinery) | definitional at support level | **no** — zero-weight events may remain in the support (allowed sections with weight 0); G0 deliberately does not assume the converse |
| Gleason/Hilbert | yes (probability-0 for orthogonal/impossible events) | derived from geometry, not assumed; measure-zero subtleties exist |
| GPT cones | yes via cone boundary | no (boundary ≠ empty) |
| CT probability | possibility is task-level; zero weight is a derived decision property | not assumed |

**Load-bearing for GRUT:** the p=0-but-possible gap is exactly where a future
possibility/statistics law could place content — and it is where the standard frameworks
are silent or definitional.

## 8. Overall verdict

All nontrivial possibility→statistics constraints in the known frameworks enter by
**supplying structure beyond the compatibility complex** (event relations, geometry,
cones, superinformation conditions). G0's computation confirms this structurally at
levels G0-B/G0-C/D and documents the comparator pattern. The one genuinely G0-specific
output is the exact accounting of *where* the freedom lives and how much special support
would be needed to remove it.
