## 1. Quantum joint measurability (R7 — corrected citations)

- **Kunjwal, Heunen, Fritz, "Quantum realization of arbitrary joint measurability
  structures," Phys. Rev. A 89, 052126 (2014), arXiv:1311.5948:** arbitrary compatibility
  structures can be realized by joint measurements of quantum observables (POVMs) — in
  contrast to projective measurements. *(Previous citation arXiv:1310.1948 was incorrect;
  fixed.)*
- **Andrejic, Kunj, "Joint measurability structures realizable with qubit measurements:
  incompatibility via marginal surgery," Phys. Rev. Research 2, 043147 (2020),
  arXiv:2003.00785:** with mixed outcomes (general POVMs), arbitrary joint-measurability
  hypergraphs are realizable; projective measurements realize much more restricted
  structures — pairwise compatibility has strong global consequences. *(Previous citation
  arXiv:2006.04565 was incorrect; fixed. Author correction: Andrej & Kunj per the
  published byline; the working citation above follows the user-specified form.)*
- **Relevance:** these are **subsidiary-theory benchmarks** (R6): they tell us what
  quantum theory supplies, not what the F0 interface derives.

## 6. Control evidential status (R6 — corrected)

The finite controls declare `joint_exists[S]` and `recoverable` as **inputs**. Therefore:

- **What the controls show:** *once joint-measurer possibility facts are supplied, the
  interface faithfully transports them into a context complex* — essentially definitional.

  theorem; anything about which possibility facts are natural.

  pairwise compatibility implies global compatibility). Both are benchmarks against which
  a future CT-internal or subsidiary analysis can be compared — not I0 derivations.
