# GGC0 G2 A-7 GENERATIVE SEARCH — CHARTER 01

## 1. Authorized target

The only authorized G2 target is:

> **A-7 — outcome-selection rule / Born measure**

No other Ledger-A item may be searched, split, reopened or used as an
alternative generative target.

The target remains one frozen A-7 unit, but G2 tracks two obligations for
testing purposes:

- **O — definite outcome:** a single realized outcome rather than only
  decohered branches;
- **B — Born weights:** ensemble probabilities obey the Born measure for
  admissible quantum states.

These are testing obligations, not a reclassification or split of A-7.

A positive A-7 result requires **both O and B**.

## 2. Central G2 question

> Is there a minimal law on the full influence structure that produces
> definite outcomes and Born weights without supplying an outcome rule or
> Born measure as an independent primitive, and that is not merely standard
> quantum/open-system machinery in new notation?

## 3. Frozen parent requirements

Every candidate remains subject to `GRUT_GENERATIVE_CORE_0_CHARTER.md`.

### GGC0-P

The candidate must be formulated on the **full influence structure /
hierarchy**, at a declared scope. Appending a standalone collapse equation,
stochastic Schrödinger equation, hidden-variable law or probability postulate
that is not generated from the full influence structure fails GGC0-P.

### GGC0-R

The candidate must entail at least one concrete relation among
influence/response quantities not implied by the hostile baseline at the same
scope. Producing an outcome rule alone is insufficient for a positive GGC0
result.

The standing first relation target `Σ₀ = μ₀/2` remains available as a check,
but it may not be assumed.

## 4. Born obligation

For an initial state represented schematically by amplitudes `α_i`, a
candidate may use the state amplitudes as state data.

It may **not** insert `p_i = |α_i|²` as a primitive measure, sampling rule,
equilibrium postulate, typicality measure or definition and then claim the
Born rule was derived.

A valid derivation must explain why the candidate's dynamics/structure yields
the Born measure rather than an alternative normalized function of the
amplitudes. The hostile control must include non-Born alternatives.

## 5. Outcome obligation

Decoherence alone is not a definite outcome. Branch suppression,
diagonalization of a reduced density matrix, pointer-basis selection,
environmental record formation, or effective classicality do not discharge O
unless the candidate supplies a mathematically explicit mechanism by which
one realized outcome obtains. Merely reinterpreting branches does not count
unless the interpretation itself yields the required single-outcome content
without importing a new selection postulate.

## 6. No-relocation rule

A candidate fails if it merely relocates A-7 into a renamed primitive.
Examples include:

- an unexplained stochastic noise law chosen to collapse branches;
- an unexplained sampling measure;
- an unexplained hidden-variable distribution;
- an unexplained nonlinear coefficient selected to reproduce Born weights;
- an outcome selector introduced as boundary data;
- a rule whose free function is fitted to `|α|²`.

Such a candidate is: **A7-RELOCATED — not derived**.

## 7. Hostile baselines

Each candidate must be explicitly compared, at matched scope, with relevant
established mechanisms, including where applicable:

- ordinary unitary quantum mechanics plus decoherence;
- quantum trajectories / stochastic unravelings;
- objective-collapse structures such as GRW/CSL-type mechanisms;
- hidden-variable / Bohm-type outcome mechanisms;
- Everett/decoherent-branch approaches to probability;
- standard open-system influence-functional machinery;
- Schwinger–Dyson / 2PI/nPI self-consistency;
- functional RG;
- Mori–Zwanzig / Nakajima–Zwanzig reductions.

The question is not whether GRUT can reproduce something one of these
frameworks already does. The question is whether the candidate contains a
nonreducible generative relation satisfying GGC0-P/R.

## 8. Candidate disposition

Every candidate receives exactly one disposition:

- **A7-POSITIVE:** derives both O and B, satisfies GGC0-P and GGC0-R, and
  survives the hostile baseline.
- **A7-PARTIAL-O:** definite outcome mechanism only; Born weights remain
  supplied.
- **A7-PARTIAL-B:** Born weighting structure only; no definite outcome.
- **A7-RELOCATED:** A-7 has merely been renamed/reintroduced as a new
  primitive.
- **RESTATED:** equivalent at declared scope to hostile-baseline machinery.
- **GGC0-R-FAIL:** O/B may be reproduced but no new influence/response
  relation is entailed.
- **UNFORMULABLE:** full-influence law cannot be specified without consuming
  A-7 at the level it is supposed to generate.
- **NO-CANDIDATE:** no candidate survives the declared search class.

No other positive label is permitted.

## 9. Candidate search discipline

Before detailed calculation, every candidate must state:

1. mathematical object being evolved or constrained;
2. precise place the full influence hierarchy enters;
3. source of randomness, if any;
4. whether the dynamics is deterministic or stochastic;
5. how a single outcome is defined;
6. how probabilities are calculated;
7. which quantities are supplied inputs;
8. candidate-specific GGC0-R consequence;
9. closest hostile baseline;
10. explicit falsifier.

Any candidate unable to answer all ten is rejected before further
development.

## 10. Forbidden moves

G2 may not:

- reopen another A-n target;
- split A-7 for classification purposes;
- import BRI1 as a premise;
- use TRUE COMPRESSION as evidence;
- use Card #1;
- fit cosmological data;
- alter the Born target after seeing a result;
- add a new selector because an earlier candidate fails;
- call decoherence an outcome;
- call `|α|²` derived if it entered as a measure or sampling assumption;
- count elegance or self-consistency alone as GGC0-R;
- claim a theory of reality from a G2 success.

## 12. G2 terminal

Exactly one overall terminal is permitted:

### G2-A7-PASS

At least one candidate is A7-POSITIVE.

### G2-A7-PARTIAL

At least one candidate earns a genuine O or B result but none satisfies all
A7-POSITIVE conditions.

### G2-A7-N0

The declared candidate class is exhausted and all candidates are RESTATED,
RELOCATED, GGC0-R-FAIL or otherwise negative.

### G2-A7-UNF

The search demonstrates that a law on the full influence structure cannot be
formulated without consuming A-7 or an equivalent selector/measure as a
primitive.

No positive claim follows from a partial, N0 or UNF terminal.
