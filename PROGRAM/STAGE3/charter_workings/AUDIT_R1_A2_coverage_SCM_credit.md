## 4. Summary

1. Lenses S, C and M are mostly carried over: 145 of 172 report and ledger items PASS on literal text, 24 are WEAKENED and 3 are UNCAUGHT. The losses come from distillation dropping v1 wording: the BP-5 order bound and Θ_std, ΔL₀ (D_sel excluded), NULL-M, "STANDARD-MECHANISM ⇒ 0", "the law's selection removes ≥ b_Π bits", NR-5's "NR-6 and NR-7 at full strength", record-level surrogates, and D_batch.
2. The new credit structure is not freeze-ready. G's Π term counts the size of the baseline set instead of the hand choices removed. It skips the standard-selector test in the branch the card controls, ignores realized multiplicity and profile, and quotients only by Aut(𝒞). A card can therefore earn up to 768 bits for structure a standard selector or symmetry already supplies (N-01 to N-05).
3. G is not tied to coupling, so a card can be COMPRESSIVE with b_J = 0 (N-08). b_J multiplies by N_cred both a single shared-constant pin (N-09) and isomorphic copies (N-10). The 60-bit decoder window, sized for v1 where Π earned nothing, now guards up to 927 bits (N-06).

Unbounded IP-4/IP-14 auditor families and the 𝒜_Π cliff can also kill honest cards. ST-10's feasibility claim is not supported by the text.
5. Before freezing: 6 BLOCKERs (F-1 to F-6) and 9 MAJORs (F-7 to F-15), with replacement wording in §3. After those fixes, the ST-10 honest card remains reachable: b_J ≈ 480 + G ≈ 447 against a price of about 640 plus vocabulary.
