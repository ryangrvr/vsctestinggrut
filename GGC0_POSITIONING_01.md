# GGC0 POSITIONING 01 — Infrastructure for the Ultimate Question
## Created 2026-10-04 · read-only position statement · no new physics claimed

**The ultimate question.** Can the GRUT record be completed — by a generative
law on influence structures, or otherwise — into a theory of reality? This
document states how the project's infrastructure is positioned to answer
that, and the exact gates between here and there.

**Anti-overclaim clause (binding).** The record's own floor governs: "no
candidate fundamental theory is justified" (`GRUT_PROGRAM_CLOSURE_01.md:160-162`),
"zero confirmed novel quantitative predictions; no external human peer review"
(`GRUT_PROGRAM_STATE_SYNTHESIS_01.md:164-165`). Nothing below upgrades any
status. The honest current answer to the ultimate question is **OPEN, with
specific named gates** — which is strictly stronger than "no," because the
gates are checkable.

---

## 1. What the infrastructure already guarantees

The project is positioned to answer the ultimate question because it has
already built the three things any claim of a theory of reality must survive:

1. **A dependency map that cannot be cheated.** `GRUT_WORKING_THEORY_SYNTHESIS_01.md`
   §5 records every certified derivation (a1–a14) and every certified
   non-implication (n1–n18), each cited to a ruling. A theory-of-reality
   claim can be checked against it line by line: any claim that conflicts
   with an n-entry is dead on arrival; any claim that satisfies the map is
   at least consistent with everything the program earned.
2. **A residual register with admission contracts.** `GGC0_RESIDUAL_REGISTER_01.md`
   prices every supplied layer (L1–L9) with: exact recorded status,
   classification (currently UNCLASSIFIED), the *specific* artifact that
   would change its status, and the conditional-derivation cost of its
   being supplied. This converts "the theory has 16 inputs" into a finite,
   contract-bound work list.
3. **A classification discipline that runs before any generative search.**
   The criterion (`GGC0_LAW_SOLUTION_CLASSIFICATION_CHARTER_01.md`, PROPOSED
   as amended) is frozen before application; the paper-derivation firewall
   (GGC0-R) remains governing in the parent GGC0 charter. This is the
   mechanism that decides what the ultimate question's answer would even
   have to explain.

## 2. The gate chain from here to a theory of reality

Each gate is a pre-registered, two-sided question. A gate can fail; a
failure is itself a recorded result, not a defeat.

- **G1 — Classification.** Owner ratifies the criterion; Ledger A
  is classified **atomically (A-1…A-16)** with Q1/Q2/Q3 answers and
  adversarial counterarguments, then tallied and rolled up (never over-written)
  by the L1–L9 layers. The terminal is purely mechanical:
  *G1-PASS:* at least one item has Q1 = LAW-LIKE or Q2 = LAW-LIKE; G2 opens
  only on atomic LAW-LIKE and GENERATIVE-MIXED items.
  *G1-S:* no item has a LAW-LIKE Q1/Q2 component and all sixteen verdicts
  are SOLUTION-LIKE → solution-data-only at recorded scope.
  *G1-U:* no item has a LAW-LIKE Q1/Q2 component and at least one verdict is
  MIXED/UNRESOLVED → G2 does not open, and the record is **not** certified
  solution-data-only; the terminal is recorded as unresolved classification.
  The three outcomes are exhaustive and mutually exclusive; a MIXED item
  with a LAW-LIKE Q1/Q2 component produces G1-PASS, not G1-U. Both nulls
  are real structural findings.
  *Sequencing:* G1 runs entirely from the accepted in-repo Ledger-A record;
  BRI1 and the TRUE COMPRESSION accounting stay out (non-load-bearing) so no
  downstream evidence contaminates the classification.
- **G2 — Hostile-baseline search.** For each surviving target, search for a
  minimal law on influence structures that is not SD/2PI/nPI/fRG/Mori–Zwanzig
  at declared scope. *Pass:* a candidate satisfying GGC0-P and GGC0-R.
  *Fail:* GGC0-N0 — the kinematics plus baseline machinery exhausts the
  searched class.
- **G3 — Σ₀ = μ₀/2 test.** Does any G2 candidate *derive* the record's only
  law-level lock? This is the first checkable consequence, inside an already-
  earned artifact (the standing falsifier made concrete).
- **G4 — BRI1 consumption.** Import/reproduce BRI1's frozen artifacts; test
  whether the G2 candidate's consequences discriminate its recorded
  higher-cumulant phenomenon. BRI1 is a consumer here, never a premise.
- **G5 — External novelty screen.** Literature pass: is the candidate law
  genuinely absent from prior art, or REDISCOVERED-KNOWN? (The scout's
  discipline: every main result needs a novelty screen and an independent
  check.)
- **G6 — Prediction.** Only after G1–G5: derive a quantitative, distinctive
  prediction with a pre-registered falsifier. This is the first gate at
  which "theory of reality" can even be *uttered* at more than
  programmatic strength — and even then, per the record's own rules, only
  as a claim with a named falsifier and external review.

## 3. What this positioning deliberately does not do

- It does not claim the influence hierarchy is fundamental (the seed claim's
  permitted wording is the only legal phrasing).
- It does not promise G1–G6 succeed. Two of them (G2, G5) have plausible
  negative outcomes that would still advance the record.
- It does not touch the frozen TestingGRUT record, Card #1, or any
  live-side artifact.
- It does not merge the two repositories; this workspace remains the
  experimental reconstruction space.
