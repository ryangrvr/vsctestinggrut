# Phase 1 — Frozen search inventory

## 1.1 Declared mathematical search class

The class of **dynamical laws acting on the full influence structure**
(influence-functional hierarchy of a system coupled to inaccessible degrees
of freedom, with the hierarchy defined at the level of the Keldysh/backup
influence functional or equivalent generator of nested response functions)
that produce, as **consequences of their dynamics**, a definite realized
outcome and its Born-measure probability — without an appended outcome rule,
measure, sampling postulate, or hidden-variable distribution.

Formally: candidates are of the form

  dΓ/dt = F[Γ, Φ, access, state]

where Γ is the full influence hierarchy (the generator of the nested
influence functionals Γ^(n) at all orders), Φ is the accessible degrees of
freedom, access is the declared access structure, and state is the declared
quantum state — with **no additional primitive** beyond these.

# Phase 2 — Ten-point specifications

## C1: Hierarchy self-consistency closure

1. **Object evolved:** the influence hierarchy Γ = {Γ^(n)} (generator of
   nested influence functionals); accessible state Φ evolves under an
   effective equation whose self-energy is a functional of Γ.
2. **Influence hierarchy entry point:** Γ *is* the evolved object; the
   effective dynamics of Φ is derived from Γ's self-consistency.
3. **Source of randomness:** none inserted. Any stochasticity must emerge
   from the closure.
4. **Deterministic/stochastic:** deterministic as specified.
5. **Single outcome definition:** none derivable — the self-consistency
   closure produces an effective *density matrix* or *influence-averaged
   trajectory*, not a single realized branch. No mechanism within
   self-consistency distinguishes one outcome from the ensemble. **[Fails at
   specification.]**
6. **Probability calculation:** would inherit the Born measure from the
   density matrix's diagonal — but this is the measure we are trying to
   derive, not use.
7. **Supplied inputs:** Γ structure, access, initial state — all permitted.
8. **GGC0-R consequence:** none identifiable at this stage; the candidate
   fails before this point.
9. **Nearest hostile baseline:** self-consistent Born approximation / 2PI
   effective action — exactly this structure.
10. **Falsifier:** any demonstration that the closure produces a single
    realized outcome; none exists.

**Specification verdict: FAILS AT ITEM 5.** Self-consistency produces
influence-averaged dynamics, not actualization. The candidate does not reach
detailed calculation.

## C2: Influence-noise equivalence closure

1. **Object evolved:** Γ, with the accessible Φ coupled to it.
2. **Influence hierarchy entry point:** Γ determines the statistics of the
   effective noise felt by Φ — if the noise is *derived from* Γ rather than
   inserted.
3. **Source of randomness:** the hypothesis is that Γ's closure *generates*
   effective stochasticity. But there is a fundamental problem: a
   deterministic law on Γ produces deterministic dynamics; to get
   *stochastic* dynamics with specific noise correlations, the correlations
   must come from somewhere. The only source within the declared class is Γ
   itself — but Γ is the influence structure, not a random variable.
   **[Problem identified at specification.]**
4. **Deterministic/stochastic:** the candidate needs stochastic dynamics but
   cannot insert a noise law without relocating A-7 (charter §6: "an
   unexplained stochastic noise law chosen to collapse branches" is
   explicitly listed as relocation).
5. **Single outcome definition:** would require the noise to collapse the
   state — but the noise statistics themselves are undetermined without an
   additional primitive.
6. **Probability calculation:** would follow from noise statistics, which are
   not derivable without insertion.
7. **Supplied inputs:** Γ, Φ, access, state — all permitted, but insufficient.
8. **GGC0-R consequence:** none identifiable.
9. **Nearest hostile baseline:** quantum trajectories / stochastic
   unravelings — where the noise IS inserted (different unravelings give
   different physics, same reduced dynamics). The candidate's premise is that
   Γ uniquely fixes the unraveling; this is not established by any known
   structure.
10. **Falsifier:** demonstration that Γ's closure uniquely fixes a noise law
    that produces definite outcomes; not derivable from the declared class.

**Specification verdict: FAILS AT ITEMS 3–4.** The stochasticity required for
outcome selection cannot be generated from a deterministic law on Γ without
either inserting a noise law (relocation) or leaving it undefined (no
mechanism). Terminated as **A7-RELOCATED** (if noise inserted) or fails
specification (if not).

## C3: Access-relative actualization

1. **Object evolved:** the joint (Γ, Φ) pair, with access as a structural
   ingredient.
2. **Influence hierarchy entry point:** Γ couples to Φ; access selects which
   parts of Φ are "real" for the observer.
3. **Source of randomness:** none inserted; the candidate's premise is that
   access-relativity generates the appearance of stochasticity.
4. **Deterministic/stochastic:** deterministic in the (Γ, Φ, access) state
   space; stochastic only in the observer's description.
5. **Single outcome definition:** "the outcome is what the access structure
   records." But this is relational actualization: it says the outcome is
   defined *relative to* the access. The charter requires "one realized
   outcome" — and the candidate's answer is that realization is
   access-relative. This is a definitional move, not a dynamical mechanism.
   **[Problem at item 5.]**
6. **Probability calculation:** the probability of recording outcome i would
   be determined by... what? The candidate has no mechanism that produces
   |α_i|² rather than any other function of the amplitudes. The access
   structure does not intrinsically weight amplitudes.
7. **Supplied inputs:** Γ, Φ, access, state. The access structure would have
   to do the work of selecting outcomes *and* generating the Born weights —
   but access is a structural input, not a measure.
8. **GGC0-R consequence:** none identifiable without assuming the Born
   weights as an access property (relocation).
9. **Nearest hostile baseline:** Everett / decoherent-branch probability —
   where the same problem (deriving Born from branch structure alone) is
   well-known and unresolved at this level.
10. **Falsifier:** demonstration that access-relative dynamics produces Born
    weights without inserting them; not derivable.

**Specification verdict: FAILS AT ITEM 6.** Access-relativity can define
*what* an outcome is but cannot derive *which* probability weights the
outcomes have without inserting the Born measure into the access structure's
properties. Terminated as **A7-RELOCATED** (if access is given Born-measure
properties) or fails specification (if not).

## C4: Hierarchy instability / attractor selection

1. **Object evolved:** Γ's dynamics, with attractors corresponding to
   different outcomes.
2. **Influence hierarchy entry point:** Γ's nonlinear dynamics; attractors
   in Γ-space correspond to different definite outcomes.
3. **Source of randomness:** none inserted; which attractor is reached is
   determined by initial conditions in Γ-space.
4. **Deterministic/stochastic:** deterministic; the "randomness" of outcomes
   comes from sensitivity to initial conditions in Γ.
5. **Single outcome definition:** the attractor reached. This is a genuine
   mechanism — one attractor, one outcome, deterministically selected by
   Γ's initial state.
6. **Probability calculation:** the probability of reaching attractor i is
   the measure of the basin of attraction i in Γ's initial-condition space.
   **Problem:** what measure on Γ's initial-condition space? The candidate
   must supply a measure to compute probabilities — and the Born measure on
   amplitudes must emerge from the Γ-space measure. The natural candidate
   (the "natural" or "uniform" measure on Γ's initial conditions) does not
   in general induce |α_i|² on outcomes. To make it do so requires either
   (a) fine-tuning the Γ-dynamics so that the natural measure pushes forward
   to Born — an unexplained nonlinear coefficient, charter-listed
   relocation; or (b) inserting the Born measure on Γ's initial conditions —
   direct relocation.
7. **Supplied inputs:** Γ, Φ, access, state, plus (implicitly) the
   initial-condition measure on Γ.
8. **GGC0-R consequence:** none identifiable without the measure insertion.
9. **Nearest hostile baseline:** nonlinear collapse mechanisms with
   amplification (GRW/CSL-type) — where the noise IS inserted with specific
   statistics chosen to give Born.
10. **Falsifier:** demonstration that a natural measure on Γ's initial
    conditions induces Born weights without tuning; not derivable in
    general.

**Specification verdict: FAILS AT ITEM 6.** The candidate has a genuine
outcome mechanism (attractor selection) but cannot derive the Born weights
without inserting a measure on Γ's initial conditions whose pushforward is
|α_i|² — which is charter-defined relocation. **A7-RELOCATED** (or, if the
measure is refused, the candidate has no probability mechanism: fails O+B
jointly).

## C5: Measure-operator feedback loop

1. **Object evolved:** coupled Γ ↔ Φ dynamics with feedback in both
   directions.
2. **Influence hierarchy entry point:** Γ acts on Φ (influence); Φ acts back
   on Γ (feedback). The closed loop is the candidate's structural innovation.
3. **Source of randomness:** none inserted.
4. **Deterministic/stochastic:** deterministic in the joint (Γ, Φ) space.
5. **Single outcome definition:** the fixed point (or periodic orbit) of the
   closed feedback loop, corresponding to a definite outcome.
6. **Probability calculation:** the probability of reaching fixed point i is
   determined by the basin structure of the closed loop in joint space —
   same measure problem as C4, now compounded: the measure must be specified
   on the *joint* (Γ, Φ) initial-condition space, and its pushforward must
   yield Born. No natural measure does this in general.
7. **Supplied inputs:** Γ, Φ, access, state, plus joint initial-condition
   measure.
8. **GGC0-R consequence:** none identifiable.
9. **Nearest hostile baseline:** self-consistent dressed-matrix / closed
   self-consistent dynamics (related to 2PI but with a feedback loop); not a
   standard mechanism for outcome selection.
10. **Falsifier:** demonstration that the closed loop's natural joint-space
    measure yields Born weights without tuning; not derivable.

**Specification verdict: FAILS AT ITEM 6** for the same structural reason as
C4, with the additional issue that the feedback structure, while genuinely
non-standard, does not solve the measure problem. **A7-RELOCATED** or fails
jointly.

# Phase 8 — Final G2 terminal

## The structural result (exhaustion criterion, §1.3)

The common failure point of all five candidates is the **measure problem**:
the declared class is

  dΓ/dt = F[Γ, Φ, access, state]   (no additional primitive)

and the question is whether such an F can produce, as a consequence of its
dynamics, both (O) a single realized outcome and (B) the Born distribution
over outcomes.

The structural result:

> **Any deterministic law F on the influence hierarchy that selects a
> definite outcome defines a map from initial conditions to outcomes. The
> probability of an outcome is then a property of the measure on initial
> conditions — a measure that is not determined by F itself. To obtain the
> Born distribution |α_i|², the measure must either be inserted with exactly
> the right structure (relocation, per charter §6) or F must be fine-tuned
> so that a "natural" measure pushes forward to Born (relocation by
> fine-tuning, charter §6 items 4 and 6). No F in the declared class
> escapes this dilemma, because the dilemma is not a property of any
> particular F — it is a property of the relationship between deterministic
> dynamics and the measure needed to define probabilities.**

This is the no-go argument that constitutes the exhaustion criterion: the
class has been searched, not sampled, because the failure is structural —
any candidate in the declared form (deterministic law on Γ with no
additional primitive) faces the same measure dilemma regardless of its
specific structure.

**The stochastic variant** (if F is allowed to be stochastic) does not
escape: the noise law's statistics must be specified, and those statistics
either reproduce Born (inserted, relocation) or don't (no Born derivation).
This is C2's failure, generalized.

**The access-relative variant** (C3) does not escape: access can define what
an outcome *is* but cannot weight outcomes without Born-measure properties
being inserted into the access structure.
