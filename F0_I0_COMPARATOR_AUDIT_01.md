# F0 I0 — COMPARATOR AUDIT 01
**Status:** campaign artifact. Targeted audit of the null comparators (charter §6),
deciding for each: *is the F0-I0 interface already exactly this object?* NOT FOUND ≠ DOES
NOT EXIST.

## 1. Quantum joint measurability

- **Kunjwal, Heunen, Fritz, "Quantum realization of arbitrary joint measurability
  structures," Phys. Rev. A 89, 052126 (2014); arXiv:1310.1948:** every (abstract)
  compatibility structure — an arbitrary collection of jointly measurable subsets — can be
  realized by joint measurements of quantum observables. Verified claim (consistent with
  the campaign baseline).
- **Andrejic, Kunjwal, "Joint measurability structures realized by measurements with
  mixed outcomes," Phys. Rev. Research 2, 043147 (2020); arXiv:2006.04565:** with mixed
  outcomes (general POVMs), **arbitrary joint-measurability hypergraphs** are realizable;
  projective (PVM) measurements realize only much more restricted structures — pairwise
  compatibility has strong global consequences.
- **Relevance:** the *target output* of the F0 interface (a downward-closed joint
  measurability complex, including the Specker triangle) is exactly a realizable object of
  standard quantum measurement theory. Any F0 interface that constructs `C_CT(M)` via
  joint-measurer + classical post-processing is building the **same mathematics**.

## 2. Specker / sharpness controls (frozen distinctions)

- Sharp/projective sector: pairwise compatibility ⇒ strong global consequences (Vorob'ev-
  type; graph restrictions); the structural Specker property lives here (charter §3.1).
- Unsharp/POVM sector: pairwise-without-triplewise is generically realizable (the K2
  complex). The two sectors remain distinct; the interface candidate must not blur them.

## 3. Effect algebras

Effect-algebra compatibility (joint measurability of effects within an effect algebra;
unsharp quantum effects form effect algebras) is an established abstract home of
partial-measurement compatibility. **Overlap with the F0 interface:** the induced
compatibility relation among measurer-variables is functionally an effect-algebra-style
compatibility. **Verdict:** the abstract interface object is known mathematics; F0's use
of it must be labeled as such.

## 4. Partial Boolean algebras / commeasurability

Partial Boolean algebras formalize "commeasurable" families of propositions with
Boolean joins/joins only within compatible blocks; compatibility complexes from
commeasurability are standard (Kochen–Specker contextuality literature). **Verdict:**
another known formalization of the same target; the F0 interface candidate at the
sharp/propositional scope would restate this.

## 5. Sheaf/contextuality scenarios

The Exec-01 empirical-model complex (Abramsky–Brandenburger, B5) is the target output
carrier: contexts = jointly implementable measurement sets; downward-closed by declared
convention; compatibility = sheaf-gluing structure. **Verdict:** the interface maps
*into* this standard carrier; no novelty in the carrier itself (F0-A was already RESTATED
against it in Exec-01).

## 6. Level analysis (I0-A / I0-B / I0-C) against the comparators

- **I0-A (CT alone):** base CT theorems (complementary impossibility in superinformation
  media; fine-grain measurability) **under-determine the middle regime** — they fix
  impossibility for complementary pairs and possibility along coarse-grainings, but do
  not decide, e.g., three pairwise-compatible non-complementary measurers with no triple.
  Base CT has no unsharp/partial-measurement calculus (baseline §3). **Level I0-A alone is
  insufficient to reproduce the K2 complex** — CT base principles, as audited, encode a
  *pairwise incompatibility* notion (complementarity) but no *higher-order joint
  measurability* hierarchy.
- **I0-B (CT + subsidiary):** with quantum measurement theory as subsidiary, the
  possibility facts (which joint measurers exist) are supplied by the subsidiary theory's
  joint-measurability theorems — the full hypergraph hierarchy returns. This is the
  coherent architecture: **CT language + subsidiary possibility facts → contextual
  compatibility complex.** Priced: subsidiary-theory possibility facts (e.g. quantum
  joint-measurability conditions) are `SUPPLIED` by the subsidiary theory, not CT-derived.
- **I0-C (imported operational compatibility):** the declared interface definition
  (joint measurer + classical post-processing + restriction consistency) is the
  parent-measurement structure of standard joint measurability. At face value: **the
  interface is standard joint-measurability mathematics translated into CT terminology.**

## 7. Pairwise vs higher-order information (charter §10) — decisive finding

**Base CT's measurement principles encode a pairwise incompatibility notion
(complementarity) but no higher-order joint-measurability hierarchy.** Consequences:

1. Base CT **cannot distinguish** three pairwise-compatible measurers with a triple joint
   measurer from the Specker triangle without one — the distinguishing information is
   exactly what quantum joint-measurability theory (and its POVM machinery) supplies.
2. Therefore a compatibility **graph** must not be promoted to a context **hypergraph** at
   base-CT scope. Any K2-type complex requires subsidiary-theory input (or an
   I0-constructed extension of CT, which is not authorized here).
3. This is a **structural fact about the interface**, and it drives the terminal.
