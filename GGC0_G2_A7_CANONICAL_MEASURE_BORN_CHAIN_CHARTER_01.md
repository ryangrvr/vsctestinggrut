# GGC0 G2 A-7 CANONICAL MEASURE–BORN CHAIN — CHARTER 01

## 1. Scope

This is a narrow successor within G2.

The sole target remains:

> **A-7 — outcome-selection rule / Born measure**

Do not reopen any other Ledger-A item. Do not rerun C1–C5. Do not generate a
general new candidate inventory. Do not open G3.

The sole question is:

> Can a law on the full influence hierarchy structurally select a canonical
> measure and outcome map such that their pushforward is the Born measure,
> without Born information being encoded upstream?

## 2. Frozen chain

The object of study is exactly:

`F` → `μ_F` → `h_F` → `(h_F)_* μ_F` → `|α_i|²`.

Where:

- `F` = law acting on the full influence hierarchy;
- `μ_F` = measure selected canonically by F plus admissible structural
  conditions;
- `h_F` = dynamically earned outcome map;
- `(h_F)_* μ_F` = induced measure over realized outcomes.

Every arrow is audited separately. No arrow may be skipped.

## 3. Five gates

### M1 — Law gate

Specify the admissible mathematical class of `F`. The class must genuinely
act on the full influence hierarchy and satisfy the parent GGC0-P
requirement. No standalone probability or collapse rule may be appended.

### M2 — Canonical-measure gate

Determine whether F plus an independently motivated structural requirement
selects `μ_F`. The following do NOT suffice:

- existence of some invariant measure;
- choosing one convenient invariant measure;
- inserting an initial distribution;
- choosing a measure because it produces Born;
- fitting a free functional to Born.

A successful M2 route requires a declared uniqueness/physicality principle
that existed before examining the Born output.

### M3 — Outcome-map gate

Derive `h_F` from the same physical structure. `h_F` must map admissible
microscopic/full-hierarchy states to a single realized outcome. Decoherence
or branch labeling alone does not satisfy M3.

### M4 — Born-pushforward gate

Prove or derive: `(h_F)_* μ_F(i) = |α_i|²` for the declared admissible class
of quantum states. Numerical agreement for selected amplitudes is
insufficient. The mechanism must explain why the quadratic rule is forced
rather than merely allowed.

### M5 — Independence / no-smuggling gate

Demonstrate that Born information did not enter through:

- coefficients of F;
- definition of the measure class;
- structural restriction used to select μ_F;
- outcome map h_F;
- access structure;
- initial/boundary conditions;
- choice of coordinates or reference volume;
- an implicit quantum-equilibrium postulate.

Failure at M5 is `A7-RELOCATED`.

## 4. Preregistered structural families

Only the following structural routes may be investigated in this campaign:

### S1 — Equivariance + locality/functional restriction

> Does the Γ-dynamics admit a uniquely selected equivariant measure within
> an independently declared local/functional class?

Bohmian mechanics is a hostile comparator only. Its result may not be
imported.

### S2 — Physical/SRB-type global measure

> Does an admissible Γ-dynamics possess a genuinely global physical measure
> capable of assigning outcome frequencies across the relevant outcome
> basins?

Separate SRB measures on separate attractors do not solve this by
themselves.

### S3 — Unique-ergodic / unique-invariant structure

> Can the admissible full-hierarchy dynamics possess a uniquely
> distinguished invariant measure, and if so does its outcome pushforward
> have the required state-dependent Born form?

Do not confuse time-average convergence with arbitrary ensemble convergence.

### S4 — Symmetry / covariance / conservation selection

> Can independently motivated symmetries, locality, covariance, conservation
> or composition requirements uniquely constrain the admissible measure to
> one whose outcome pushforward is Born?

The structural requirement must be stated before inspecting its Born
consequence.

No fifth rescue family may be added during execution. A genuinely distinct
route discovered later is `OUT-OF-SCOPE SUCCESSOR`.

## 5. Required hostile control

For every route compare against a normalized non-Born family, for example
schematically:

`p_i(q) = |α_i|^q / Σ_j |α_j|^q`, with `q ≠ 2`.

The analysis must identify what specifically excludes the non-Born family.
Merely verifying that `q=2` works is not a derivation.

## 6. Composition requirement

Where applicable, test whether the proposed rule respects composition of
independent systems. If a structural condition singles out the quadratic
rule only after imposing multiplicativity/additivity/composition, record
exactly which assumption does the work. If that assumption is equivalent to
Born weighting, mark relocation.

## 7. State-dependence requirement

A fixed invariant measure on Γ-space is not automatically Born. The campaign
must explain how the canonical measure depends appropriately on the quantum
state while remaining selected by F rather than separately supplied for each
state. This is a mandatory attack.

## 8. No historical-design inference

A comparator theory having a Born-compatible measure does not establish that
its dynamics independently derived that measure. Distinguish:

- mathematical uniqueness given a fixed dynamics;
- physical explanation of why that dynamics is the correct one;
- historical or deliberate co-design around Born.

Only the first may be imported as hostile mathematical precedent. None
counts as GRUT derivation.

## 9. GGC0-R remains binding

Even a successful O+B chain is insufficient for an overall GGC0 positive
result unless the candidate entails a concrete new relation among
influence/response quantities not already implied by the hostile baseline.
The campaign must therefore record whether any successful structural route
supplies a candidate GGC0-R relation. Do not invent or fit such a relation
after obtaining Born.

## 11. Route dispositions

Each S-route receives exactly one:

- **CHAIN-POSITIVE** — M1–M5 pass and both O/B are genuinely derived.
- **MEASURE-ONLY** — a canonical μ_F is earned, but outcome/Born pushforward
  is not.
- **OUTCOME-ONLY** — h_F is earned, but canonical Born statistics are not.
- **BORN-ONLY** — Born pushforward structure is earned but no definite
  outcome.
- **RELOCATED** — Born/outcome information entered upstream.
- **BASELINE-RESTATEMENT** — route reduces to known hostile-baseline
  machinery.
- **STRUCTURAL-FAIL** — the stated structural condition does not select the
  required object.
- **UNRESOLVED** — available mathematics is insufficient to decide the route
  without inventing a new premise.

Exactly one label per route.

## 12. Campaign terminal

Exactly one overall terminal:

### G2-A7-CHAIN-PASS

At least one route is CHAIN-POSITIVE and also supplies a viable GGC0-R
consequence.

### G2-A7-CHAIN-PARTIAL

At least one route earns a genuine nontrivial portion of the chain but none
satisfies the full positive condition.

### G2-A7-CHAIN-N0

All four preregistered structural routes fail, relocate, or reduce to
baseline, **and** a route-specific argument establishes exhaustion of the
declared S1–S4 campaign. This terminal applies only to S1–S4, not to all
conceivable deterministic laws.

### G2-A7-CHAIN-OPEN

No positive result is earned, but at least one route remains mathematically
unresolved or the declared routes cannot legitimately be exhausted. This is
a valid terminal and is not a failure of the campaign.
