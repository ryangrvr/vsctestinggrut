**Permitted inputs.** (i) The earned kinematic admissibility record

**Non-negotiable hostile baseline.** Every candidate law must be diffed
against the pre-existing machinery that already satisfies the intuitive
requirements: Schwinger–Dyson self-consistency; self-consistent 2PI/nPI
effective actions; self-consistent Born approximations; functional RG;
Mori–Zwanzig/Nakajima–Zwanzig with back-acting projected dynamics. A candidate
that reduces to (unitary microscopic dynamics + any partition + any access)
with a relabeled influence object is **RESTATED, not GRUT**, and is recorded
as such. This is the program's own standing indictment
(`GRUT_FINAL_SYNTHESIS_01.md:152-154`) turned into a kill-condition.

**Pre-registered success criteria (both must hold for any positive claim).**
- GGC0-P: the candidate law is formulated on the full influence data
  (hierarchy level, not a single kernel), with a declared scope.
- GGC0-R: the law entails at least one **relation among influence/response
  coefficients** not implied by the hostile baseline at the same scope.
  Concrete first target: does any candidate *derive* the recorded
  conditional law-level lock **Σ₀ = μ₀/2**
  (`SATURATION_CERTIFICATION_02.md`; FRONTIER_QUEUE K7), rather than merely
  admitting it as a solution?

**Pre-registered failure criteria (equally informative).**
- GGC0-N0: after the declared candidate-class search, every admissible law on
  influence structures examined reduces to the hostile baseline at declared
  scope. Then the earned statement is: *"the kinematic constraints plus
  baseline machinery exhaust the searched class; no nonreducible generative
  law exists in that class"* — a structural terminal, not a silence.
- GGC0-UNF: the law notion itself is unformulable without consuming a
  supplied input (partition, access, generator, orientation) at the level it
  is supposed to generate. Then the earned statement mirrors EA-0/RAI: the
  generative law, like access, may be structurally supplied — and Ledger A
  stands as solution data with the record's support.
